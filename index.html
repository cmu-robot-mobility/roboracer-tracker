Qualifying

How completion is scored
  • Evaluations: a team can do more than one timed evaluation. The team picks one, and only that one counts for best lap, completion and seeding.
  • Two crash-free laps in a row = 100%. Fewer laps scale completion down: 1.5 laps = 75%, 1 lap = 50%.
  • Scrapes: the first two are free, then −5% each.
  • Crashes: −10% each.
  • To advance to the race a car must pass obstacle avoidance (2 laps in a row), and its chosen evaluation must have 2 crash-free laps in a row with no crashes. It also has to meet the LiDAR visibility rule: 12 cm of body between 10 and 25 cm above the ground.
  • Seeding is by best lap time (in seconds) among teams that advance. The fastest lap is shown in purple.
